## Causality Audit

- F0 survival pressure creates incomplete mission approval.
- Incomplete approval creates an off-target arrival and missing local anchor.
- The off-target arrival forces patient escort and local trust-building.
- Patient records create contradiction with the official target narrative.
- Investigation confirms the reformer's crimes but disproves the official causal interpretation.
- Return deadline, royal siege, and F0 mortality figures force a non-retryable decision.
- The target's death unlocks royal control of the western record network.
- The successful return lands in F1 and generates Volume 2's identity and evidence problem.

Result: Every Subact resolution directly creates the next problem.

## Character Agency Audit

- Aiden cannot approve, navigate, synchronize, or guarantee return alone.
- Ria warns but does not cancel the mission for him.
- Iris assists patients, not Aiden's mission, and can withdraw the return anchor.
- The reformer admits harmful methods and refuses Aiden's unverified future promise.
- Relief knights save lives while enforcing coercive classification.
- No character becomes automatically loyal after rescue.

Result: PASS.

## Antagonist Rationality Audit

- F0 selectionists use measurable survival outcomes.
- The cathedral quarantine system has real medical and logistical benefits.
- Royal forces prioritize record control because administrative collapse threatens food, land, and defense.
- The reformer monopolizes emergency authority because decentralization is too slow during erasure events.

Result: Opposition does not depend on stupidity or pure evil.

## Time / Distance / Logistics Audit

- Mission approval is distributed.
- Arrival error causes real identity and travel problems.
- Return capacity, registered possessions, travel time, and medicine are limited.
- E020 explicitly establishes incompatible time requirements.
- Iris's anchor refusal and the return stone's remaining capacity prevent free extraction.

Result: PASS.

## Mystery Audit

- Official guilt is not simply false; the reformer committed illegal acts.
- The false interpretation concerns causality, not every fact.
- Gray bell / return-stone resonance is introduced before later use.
- The erased settlement, dual birth records, prewritten reports, and future-dated taxes share the same continuity theme.
- No late temporal rule is introduced to justify the Volume ending.

Result: PASS.

## Permanent Loss Audit

- Reformer death is locked.
- F0 relationships are not restored at the Volume ending.
- Iris remains in Era N and does not follow Aiden as a reward.
- Aiden's moral responsibility is not transferred entirely to the institution.

Result: PASS.

## Reader Experience / Anti-Repeat

- E001–006: institutional countdown and mission authorization.
- E007–012: survival, quarantine, escort, and local trust.
- E013–018: record investigation and contested evidence.
- E019–024: siege, negotiation, constrained combat, irreversible choice.
- E025: altered-future payoff.

The Volume alternates administrative thriller, road emergency, medical mystery, infiltration, ethical confrontation, and future shock. The climax is not a stronger enemy but a decision under incompatible obligations.
