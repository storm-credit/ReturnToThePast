## Final Causality Audit

The automatic overwrite creates the need to split temporal intervention from life support. The split threatens stranded agents, provincial sacrifice, and the capital wall. Refusing hidden provincial sacrifice destabilizes life support. Life-support transfer requires dismantling major relics and accepting foreign audit. The remaining return bridge requires Aiden's address, but saving him through the Blank Zone would recreate the same displaced-cost system. Address sacrifice closes forced overwrite without requiring physical death. The new network then faces an immediate distribution and disaster test, which is handled through current resources, appeals, and international aid rather than renewed time travel.
