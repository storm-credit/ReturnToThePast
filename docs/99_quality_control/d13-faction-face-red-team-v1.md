## 5. V14 14D — 7 vs 3 Authority Audit

### Potential S0
Existing institution bible says all 7 authorities are required for normal national intervention.
V14 says restorationists control 3 axes and can trigger forced overwrite.

D13 distinction:

- 7 authorities = lawful new intervention / lawful shutdown / formal restart.
- 3 axes = priority configuration inside an already-running connected central system.
- other four authorities do NOT consent to forced restoration.
- their old technical/life connections persist temporarily to prevent immediate collapse.

Therefore 3-axis capture abuses standing connections; it does not bypass the law by magically becoming a 7-signature approval.

Result: **CLOSED**.

### Why has this not allowed dictatorship every earlier volume?

Four constraints:

1. Normal conditions still have functioning local burden/audit institutions able to challenge/revoke changed operations.
2. Three-axis capture requires simultaneous control of throne purpose, cathedral synchronization and archive-address routing — difficult capital-scale seizure.
3. Priority capture only acts inside already approved/connected operational envelopes; it cannot create arbitrary new coordinates, returns or resurrection.
4. V14 is uniquely dangerous because system separation is half-finished: old central connections are still live while new regional participation states have already diverged.

Thus V14 is a transition-state exploit, not an everyday hidden superpower.

Result: **CLOSED**.

### Why doesn't independent audit simply stop it?

Audit can expose/reject legitimacy but cannot safely sever all life connections by itself.
A lawful central shutdown still requires the seven operational domains plus evacuation/local-network readiness.

This is why public audit can reveal the abuse while technical execution continues long enough to trigger E348.

Result: **CLOSED**.
