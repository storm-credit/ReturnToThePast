## GA I — 잘못된 치료

### Fun Engine
- survival deadline
- first embodied time mission
- official evidence vs lived contradiction
- assassination pressure
- altered-future return shock

### Why It Works
The protagonist and reader share a constrained false model. The first irreversible murder creates guilt that powers all later Acts.

### Hostile Risk
Too many early long-term mysteries can compete with the simple emotional engine: `Should Aiden kill Seren before the return window closes?`

### Guardrail
Every mystery clue must also affect one immediate problem: passage, treatment, trust, time, evidence or escape.

**Verdict: PASS.**

## GA II — 살아남은 미래의 권리

### Fun Engine
- erased city investigation
- restoration horror
- return-window rescue
- civil conflict between real futures
- external invasion/logistics

### Why It Works
Abstract rights are attached to family, land, defense corridor, medicine and return access. F0/F1/current residents all have something real to lose.

### Hostile Risk
Court/audit/rights terminology can turn consecutive episodes into procedural debate.

### Guardrail
Any rights decision must create a visible material consequence within 1–2 episodes: changed face/family relation, closed road, missing defense corridor, medicine shortage, blocked return, land claim, refugee movement.

**Verdict: PASS+.**

## GA III — 건국의 아홉 상처

### Fun Engine
- origin-era exploration
- construction under seasonal collapse
- multi-peoples labor and supply conflict
- myth deconstruction
- impossible city-allocation choice
- prevent monopoly without preventing the life-saving engine

### Why It Works
This Act changes genre texture instead of repeating assassination/civil-war beats. The engine's origin is a survival project, not a villain lair.

### Hostile Risk
Highest lore-load risk. V8 also reaches the series' maximum unresolved-mystery load. If characters stop acting while explaining Nine Wounds history, reader momentum drops.

### Guardrail
Lore only enters through building, evacuation, funeral evidence, supply negotiation, weather failure, labor dispute, emergency vote or permanent loss. No lecture scene gets to carry a whole reveal by itself.

**Verdict: PASS WITH STRONG EXECUTION GUARD.**

## GA IV — 세 시대의 전쟁

### Fun Engine
- simultaneous interventions
- beneficial intervention vs political abuse
- younger Aiden autonomy
- competing futures
- self-causality and identity/address loss
- physical multi-era negotiation

### Why It Works
This is the strongest time-travel-specific Act. Small legal, agricultural and recruitment interventions function as warfare without requiring mass time armies.

### Hostile Risk
Highest cognitive-load risk: O/N/F3, multiple missions, two Aidens, foreign blockades, institutional authority and partial truths can blur together.

### Guardrail
Every simultaneous mission needs four reader-visible tags:
1. who wants what;
2. what immediate life function is at stake;
3. what changes if they succeed;
4. what residue reaches Aiden.

Use maps/records/objects in-story only when they solve character problems, not as exposition UI.

**Verdict: PASS+ / COGNITIVE-LOAD GUARD REQUIRED.**

## GA V — 남길 역사

### Fun Engine
- citizenship under disappearing records
- inheritance/food conflict
- plural-record investigation
- final overwrite threat
- infrastructure separation under deadline
- Aiden's irreversible address sacrifice
- first crisis of the new system

### Why It Works
The ending answers the opening system instead of defeating a final person. It preserves actual losses and proves the successor order can function after Aiden's public disappearance.

### Hostile Risk
Highest procedural/philosophical risk. Terms such as testimony citizenship, original timeline privilege, distributed consent and audit rights can become essay dialogue.

### Guardrail
Every abstract principle must be paid by a body or daily function:
- school register
- food day/wagon count
- surgery/medicine
- household recognition
- land access
- port contract
- repair time/material ownership
- emergency response delay

E369–E375 must feel like story aftermath and live civic crisis, not a policy appendix.

**Verdict: PASS WITH FINAL-PROSE GUARD.**

## Brainstorming Decision

### Not Needed
- new world premise
- new faction inventory
- new final antagonist
- new time power
- alternative ending architecture
- replacement Grand Act

### Needed Only JIT
For a specific episode or Subact, compare at most 3–4 staging options while preserving canon:
- whose action is foregrounded;
- physical location;
- scene order;
- reveal vehicle;
- pressure clock;
- life consequence;
- hook form.

This is **craft brainstorming**, not story redesign.

## Author-Question Decision

No mandatory author interview is currently required for macro architecture.

Ask the author only when:
- two options change an event or ending;
- a new major character/faction/time ability appears structurally necessary;
- a JIT decision changes a permanent loss;
- a romance/identity reveal requires author taste rather than continuity reasoning;
- the protagonist-balance regression cannot reach target without changing event ownership.

Otherwise resolve within the frozen design and report assumptions.
