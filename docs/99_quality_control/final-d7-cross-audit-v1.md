## 4. Series Causal Chain Audit

1. Incomplete future intelligence produces the first assassination.
2. The assassination produces F1 and destroys F0 relationships.
3. F1 evidence proves the target slowed the calamity.
4. Disclosure produces F2, competing missions, and regional authority.
5. Regional authority exposes erased cities, return abandonment, and burden displacement.
6. Origin-era travel proves the engine is necessary for contemporary survival but politically capturable.
7. The Nine Wounds covenant creates rights and audits while slowing emergency action.
8. Crown authority is split, producing a richer F3 and a public time-intervention industry.
9. Public intervention creates international temporal war and self-causality conflict.
10. Self-causality destroys Aiden's legal/historical address without resetting him.
11. Multi-era audit shuts central intervention but destabilizes all life-support systems.
12. Testimony citizenship, plural records, regional consent, and asset conversion produce the final distributed system.
13. Aiden's address closes the last forced-return bridge without sacrificing the Blank Zone.
14. The first crisis of the new system is solved through appeals, burdens, present resources, and international support rather than time travel.

Result: every Grand Act is caused by the previous Act's solution. The ending resolves the same record, address, return, burden, and consent questions introduced in Volume 1.

## 5. Time-Travel Rule Audit

- Direct embodied travel only.
- One mutable timeline; altered return futures are real present worlds.
- No death reset, round, checkpoint, or infinite retry.
- Aiden has one continuous subjective life with accumulated injury, age, guilt, and relationship loss.
- Arrival requires evidence/address and can have location, date, body, language, and identity error.
- Return targets an existing future anchor, not guaranteed departure reality.
- Other versions of a person are independent persons and cannot replace the dead.
- Editing one's younger life creates address and return debt rather than an instant convenient paradox.
- Mass armies and unrestricted dates remain impossible.
- No new temporal law is introduced at the final reversal.

Result: PASS.

## 6. World-Bible Integration Audit

## 8. Opposition and Institutional Utility Audit

The following opposing systems have actual measurable benefits:

- central mission approval reduces coordinate collision
- return refusal protects the wider network
- bloodline and property law reduce fraud and support obligations
- the cathedral sustains medicine, relief, calendar, and agricultural coordination
- centralized burden allocation improves capital survival and emergency speed
- crown command improves wartime response
- public time industry prevents famine, disease, and maritime deaths
- restoration can restore harvests and erased communities
- a single canon accelerates law and administration

Their structural victims, hidden costs, exit barriers, and reform losses are also recorded. Removing or decentralizing them produces real casualties and inefficiency.

Result: no major opposition depends on stupidity or pure evil.
