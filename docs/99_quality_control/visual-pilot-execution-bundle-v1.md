# 1. C01 에이든 로엔

## V0 Shape — 4 Options

```text
Create four clearly different black-and-gray silhouette explorations for the same practical fantasy field traveler.

Core identity: medium athletic build, low center of gravity, short weatherproof outer jacket ending above mid-thigh with a diagonal hem, asymmetrical shoulder repair structure, utility belt, one side visually heavier while the opposite hand remains free, compact field-ready stance.

Option A: emphasize diagonal jacket hem.
Option B: emphasize asymmetrical shoulder load.
Option C: emphasize waist equipment structure and one empty equipment ring.
Option D: emphasize compact layered jacket and folded-map carrying space.

Neutral background, readable front or 3/4 view, no facial detail, no color coding, no dramatic action pose.

Do not use a long coat, cape, giant sword, royal armor, symmetrical hero silhouette, glowing effects, or sacred-warrior styling.
```

## V1 Face — 4 Options

```text
Create four distinct face explorations for one adult male field operative.

Core anchors: medium-width skull, angular cheekbones, visible under-eye fatigue, alert practical gaze, subtle facial asymmetry from accumulated strain, mature field-worn skin, not an idol-like youth, not a prince, not a glamorous dark hero.

Hair is secondary: medium practical length that does not obstruct vision; gray-black to silver-gray is only a soft possibility, not the identity.

Use the same neutral lighting, same lens, same expression, same background for all four options. Make the facial bone proportions meaningfully different across A/B/C/D while preserving the same role.

No celebrity likeness, no glowing eyes, no pure-white power hair, no beauty-standard clone face.
```

## V2 Integrated Concept

```text
Practical fantasy field traveler, approved V0 silhouette and approved V1 face combined. Medium athletic body, low center of gravity, short diagonally cut repaired weatherproof jacket, asymmetrical shoulder repair plate, reused metal and worn leather, layered repairs from different periods. One hand holds a folded field map. The other rests near a visibly empty equipment ring where a return tag once hung. He scans exits, floor markings and routes before people. Functional gloves, subtle cumulative wear, no decorative hero gear.

Neutral readable lighting, full-body 3/4 concept art, material separation clear.

Do not add a long black coat, giant sword, glowing eyes, royal styling, symmetric armor, unexplained scars, or iconic equipment belonging to other characters.
```

Review against C08/C29/C30 before approval.

# 2. C02 리아 세른

## V0 Shape — 4 Options

```text
Create four grayscale silhouette explorations for an adult fantasy archival field worker.

Core identity: uneven layered sleeves, vertical document case hanging beside the torso, thin overlapping record plates, visual weight centered on hands and side storage rather than shoulders, narrow functional working footprint.

A: sleeve-length asymmetry.
B: strong vertical document-case block.
C: visible negative space between two or three thin record plates.
D: asymmetrical hand-and-bag working posture.

No magic-book pose, no robe silhouette, no long-haired heroine silhouette, no combat cape.
```

## V1 Face — 4 Options

```text
Four face explorations for one adult woman archivist. Sleep-reduced concentration, practical working tension, distinct facial bone proportions, non-glamorous and non-aristocratic. Hair partly secured for document work with evidence of being retied during long shifts; hairstyle must not be the main identifier.

Same neutral light, lens and expression. Avoid ice-queen beauty coding, porcelain perfection, model-face symmetry, sorceress styling.
```

## V2 Integrated Concept

```text
Adult archival field worker with approved silhouette and face. Uneven layered sleeves, vertical provenance-tagged document case, thin translucent record plates used to compare overlapping records, wax, paper, thin metal frames, repaired cloth, ink and paper-cut wear. She aligns document corners and adds a small source point while working. The record plates look physical and archival, not holographic or magical.

No floating books, no glowing interface, no pristine white scholar costume.
```

Review against C12/C22.

# 3. R03 개혁가의 절검

## V0 Shape — 4 Options

```text
Four silhouette designs for a shortened ordinary military sword that became a record-seal cutting tool.

Core identity: blade deliberately cut through the middle, clearly shorter than a standard sword, reinforced notch or working edge for cutting seals and binding cords, repaired ordinary handle, tool-first rather than legendary-weapon silhouette.

A: angled deliberate cut.
B: stronger reinforced seal-cutting notch.
C: visible repair pins and shortened balance.
D: minimal partial sheath and strongest broken-tool negative space.

Neutral scale board. No glow, no runes, no jewels, no giant blade, no sacred-sword silhouette.
```

## V1 Material Study

```text
Ordinary military steel, repaired handle pins, residue and abrasion from cutting wax seals and binding cords, practical wear, no precious metal. Show close-up material studies of cut edge, reinforced working notch, repair pin, handle grip.
```

## V2 O/U/C/F Lineage

```text
Show the same shortened sword in four canon states on one neutral comparison board:
O Original military origin still visible in handle and proportions.
U In Use with abnormal seal-cutting wear and repaired working surfaces.
C Contested with a visible family/ownership return marker that prevents it reading as a hero trophy.
F Final as mismatched destroyed fragments accompanied by a plain destruction record; never reforged.

The final state must feel emotionally complete without becoming more powerful or more ornate.
```

# 4. B05 백지사슴

## V0 Observer Board

```text
Design one sovereign deer shown through three conflicting observer depictions plus one invariant study.

Scale: roughly a large real deer or slightly larger, naturalistic practical anatomy.

Observer A, B and C may differ in antler branching, coat pattern and small surface details, but all must preserve exactly the same hoof geometry, walking rhythm, head-turn angle, body scale and environmental interaction.

Panel D focuses only on invariant hoof shape, leg proportions and characteristic head-turn angle.

Do not make it a pure-white sacred deer, giant-antler forest god, glowing creature, cute companion, mount or pet.
```

## V1 Movement / Trace

```text
Show the deer independently choosing a route, stopping at a fixed distance and looking back at the same characteristic angle. Separate trace panel: identical hoofprints recorded by different observers despite conflicting descriptions, pressed feeding grass and a consistent movement line.

No human ownership marks, no collar, no saddle, no magical aura.
```

## V2 Environment Concept

```text
Sovereign deer in a protected feeding area where humans maintain a no-tracking rule. The deer remains autonomous and distant. Its mystery comes from contradictory observation, not divine spectacle. Natural landscape, ordinary animal fear remains possible, no chosen-hero bond.
```

# 5A. L01 아르켄 — Recommended First Background

## V0 Establishing — 4 Options

```text
Create four grayscale establishing compositions for an inhabited fantasy capital whose history and authority occupy different architectural layers.

Core elements that must coexist:
- old lower-port address markers and workers in foreground,
- vertical palace approval corridors rising behind,
- horizontal cathedral/civic institution crossing the middle,
- clustered observation towers linked by bridges,
- different historical foundations visible through repair and continued use rather than ruins.

A: foreground old-address marker strongest.
B: vertical palace versus horizontal civic institution contrast strongest.
C: observation-tower cluster and bridge negative spaces strongest.
D: workers using old place names while officials move through approval corridors strongest.

No empty postcard, no giant single castle, no generic white fantasy capital, no one-era architecture.
```

## V1 Material / Life

```text
White lime stone, brass, red sealing wax used in institutional details, old timber in the lower port, repaired masonry. Port workers still use older place-name boards under newer royal marks. Show clerks, patients, carriers and ordinary civic movement. Architecture serves people and procedure.
```

## V2 Integrated

```text
One-shot capital image where authority, record and ordinary life are readable at once. Strong grayscale structure, inhabited foreground, functional midground, historic institutional background. The city should be recognizable without relying on color.
```
