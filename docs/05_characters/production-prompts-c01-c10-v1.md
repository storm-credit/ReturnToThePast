## C01 에이든 로엔 — FIELD TRAVELER

### V0 Shape 4안 공통핵
`practical field traveler, medium athletic build rather than tall heroic build, low center of gravity, short diagonally cut weatherproof outer jacket, asymmetrical shoulder repair plate, utility belt, one side visually heavier while the opposite hand remains free, compact travel silhouette, no long coat`

4안 차이축:
- A: 사선 밑단 강함 / 작은 어깨판
- B: 어깨 비대칭 강함 / 밑단 최소
- C: 허리 장비띠와 빈 고리 음영 강조
- D: 짧은 외투 레이어와 지도 휴대 공간 강조

### V1 Face
`field-worn adult male face, medium-width skull, angular cheekbones, visible under-eye fatigue, practical alert gaze, slight asymmetry from accumulated strain, not princely, not youthful idol-like, medium hair that does not obstruct vision, gray-black to silver-gray range only as optional soft field`

### V2 Integrated
`one hand holding a folded field map, the other resting near an empty equipment ring where a return tag used to hang, worn waterproof fabric, reused metal plates, repaired gloves, narrow-route ready stance, scanning exits and floor markings before people`

### Variants
- F0 FIELD: 등록표식/귀환장비 가장 많음
- ALTERED/WORN: 여러 시대 수선재 혼합
- ADDRESS-LOSS: 공식표식 제거, 빈 패치 증가
- FINAL-ECHO: 기능 생활복 위주, C30과 동일인 암시 금지

### DO NOT
`long black coat, royal swordsman, giant sword, glowing eyes, pure white hair as power sign, symmetrical armor, elegant prince face, heroic cape, permanent sacred sword ownership`

### Collision
C08보다 무겁고 닫힘. C29보다 귀환 흔적이 현재적. C30보다 기능장비/수선계보가 강함.

`archival field worker, uneven layered sleeves, vertical document case hanging beside torso, thin overlapping frame plates, hands and side storage defining silhouette rather than shoulders, narrow functional footprint`

- A: 겹소매 길이차 중심
- B: 세로 문서갑 중심
- C: 얇은 판 2–3장 빈공간 중심

`sleep-reduced focused archivist, adult female face with concentrated working tension, non-glamorous practical expression, hair partly secured for document work with evidence of repeated retying, distinct facial proportions independent of hairstyle`

`handling thin translucent record plates and provenance-tagged document case, wax, paper cuts, ink traces, repaired cloth, aligning corners and marking source points, no magical book spectacle`

F0 PRIVATE ARCHIVE / PUBLIC EVIDENCE / FINAL PRIVATE-LOSS.

`ice queen beauty, sorceress robe, floating magic books, hologram UI, pristine white scholar costume, long flowing hair as primary identity`

### Collision
C12=분산·야외 / C22=블록 원본. 리아는 유동·겹침·출처.
