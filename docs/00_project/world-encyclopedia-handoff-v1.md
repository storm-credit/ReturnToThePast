## Maintenance Protocol

Any new world detail must record:

- Canon status: HARD / SOFT / ASSUMPTION / DEPRECATED
- era and future-state applicability
- location and institution impact
- character or faction owner
- economic and legal consequence
- E001–E375 first use and payoff
- permanent-loss compatibility
- similarity and repetition check

## Change Blocking Conditions

A proposed change is blocked when it:

- restores F0 as the single true timeline
- creates regression, reset, resurrection, or death retry
- restores a permanent loss through another time-version
- gives Aiden all temporal authorities
- turns the Nine Wounds into a numbered villain organization
- turns sovereign beasts into collectible pets
- removes the life-support cost of shutting the central engine
- reopens time travel as an easy answer after the ending
- erases current people to reward nostalgia without explicit moral and legal consequence
