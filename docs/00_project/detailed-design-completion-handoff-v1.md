## 4. Non-Negotiable Story Locks

- direct embodied time travel, not regression
- one continuous subjective life for Aiden
- altered return futures are real present worlds
- future intelligence can be incomplete or manipulated
- authority remains distributed
- no one assassination solves a civilizational fixed point
- other timeline versions do not replace the dead
- permanent losses are not silently restored
- every major victory creates the next problem
- systems retain real utility and rational supporters
- the ending closes temporal intervention without restoring F0
