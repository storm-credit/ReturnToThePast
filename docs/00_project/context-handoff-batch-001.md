# Context Handoff — Batch 001

Last Updated: 2026-08-07

## Established Foundation

- The story is direct embodied time travel, not regression.
- The protagonist has one continuous subjective life.
- The default model is one mutable timeline with overwritten futures leaving palimpsest residue.
- The future reached on return may differ from the future of departure.
- The kingdom civilization is the time engine.
- Time authority is divided across mission approval, throne access, cathedral synchronization, tower coordinates, archive address, border return, field choice and local consent.
- Death and major losses are not automatically reversible.

## Initial Dramatic Proof

- F0 sends Aiden to remove a supposed originator of the grey calamity.
- The target was actually slowing the deletion of provincial history.
- The completed mission creates F1, a more populous but militarized future.
- F1 treats Aiden as a hero, while his F0 relationships no longer exist.
- Restoring F0 would erase people who only exist in F1.

## Provisional Character Roles

- Aiden: field traveler
- Ria: palimpsest archivist candidate
- Iris: Era N local anchor candidate
- F1 commander: rational institutional opposition
- Baltazar: Era O architect candidate, never an omniscient puppet master
