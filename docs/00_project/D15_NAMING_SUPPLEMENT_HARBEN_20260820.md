## 1. Canon Name

**하르벤**

Role:
Era O 8D에서 영구 장소손실을 감수하는 변경 정착도시.

Previous functional label:
`변경도시`.

After first major on-page naming, do not reduce the city back to `변경도시` as its normal name.

---

## 2. In-World Etymology

Era O 건국연합어 내부 어원설:

- `하르` — 바깥, 끝, 경계 쪽
- `벤` — 머무는 터, 피난 정착지

Combined sense:
**`경계 끝의 머무는 곳` / `바깥 피난터`.**

This is fictional in-world etymology, not a claim about real-world languages.

---

## 3. Pronunciation

하르-벤.

Do not romanize in manuscript unless a metadata/export format requires it.

---

## 4. Canon Function

Harben is NOT:
- an empty ruin before E198
- a disposable military outpost with no civilians
- the only poor city in Era O
- completely resurrectable later

Harben IS:
- a living border/refuge settlement
- mixed in mobility and occupation
- less politically central than the capital candidate but not replaceable as a community
- permanently lost as the same place/land/building continuity after E199
- survived by evacuees, name, testimonies and portable records/materials

---

## 5. Permanent-Loss Rule

`Harben survives in memory` does not mean `Harben can be restored later`.

The following do NOT recreate the original city:
- rebuilding a similar settlement elsewhere
- reusing the name
- carrying old stones/artifacts
- preserving maps
- survivors forming a new community

This preserves V8's permanent loss and the series no-reset contract.
