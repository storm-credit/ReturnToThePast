## Locked Final Outcomes

- No regression, reset, resurrection, or complete restoration.
- F0 is not restored.
- Central temporal intervention, forced overwrite, and automatic return are closed.
- Life-support functions move to imperfect distributed regional networks.
- The Blank Zone is not sacrificed to preserve Aiden.
- Aiden may physically survive but loses public name, historical address, heroic identity, and return right.
- The first reformer, F1 commander, vanished city, Ria's private F0 memories, and part of the Blank Zone remain permanent losses.
- Major relics are dismantled, sealed, retired, or destroyed rather than accumulated as protagonist power.
- The ending rejects a new saint/king/founder cult.
- The first crisis of the new order is solved through present resources, appeal, burden sharing, and international assistance—not reopened time travel.
