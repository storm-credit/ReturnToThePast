# E370 — 동의는 철회될 수 있다

## POV
Iris.

## Problem
A region invokes its withdrawal/partial-participation clause.

### Test
Does the system:
- punish withdrawal
- shift hidden burden
- or re-negotiate load publicly?

### Result
It must re-calculate and expose who pays more.

This is slower and politically ugly.

### Cost
Another region/clinic/route temporarily carries more load until an alternative is agreed.

Do not reward refusal with zero cost.

## Hook
A school ledger excludes a child because the new system still expects a stable historical address field.

- reads the official form aloud

- guardian confirmation
- child objection

to amend the school record without inventing a past birth record.

### Permanent Principle
Right to school/service can exist without fabricating historical origin.

## Hook
