## 3. Resolved Does Not Mean Prose-Ready Forever

`RESOLVED` here means:
- Grand Act / Volume / Subact causality can proceed without inventing a new rule or choosing a new plot direction.
- named recurring faces / institutional actors are sufficient.
- world/time mechanics do not contain a blocking contradiction.

It does NOT mean every scene has:
- exact calendar date
- street name
- one-off NPC name
- exact wagon count
- exact patient count
- exact room geometry

Those belong in Context Pack / scene preflight when they affect prose.
