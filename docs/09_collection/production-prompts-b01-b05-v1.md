## B01 길등짐승 — THE MOVING ROAD

### V0 Rest Shape 4안
`large low-bodied route beast, back wide enough for two or three adults standing side by side, low stable center, no towering neck, broad continuous back line, non-monstrous scale relative to settlements`

4안 차이축:
- A: 등폭과 낮은 중심
- B: 발배치와 장거리 보행 안정
- C: 노령 개체의 겹길무늬
- D: 목/머리 축소, 등과 발자국 중심

### Moving Prompt
`walking in a long steady path, making gradual route changes long before obstacles, never leaping dramatically, luminous route-like back pattern visible only in motion, grass left leaning in the direction of passage`

### Trace Prompt
`broad evenly spaced footprints remaining after rain, flattened grass orientation, low breathing presence before body appears`

### Scale Prompt
`human group beside the beast, large working-animal scale but not city-sized kaiju`

### Contract Scene
`humans removing military stakes and noise markers from the route, beast choosing to pass nearby on its own, no harness or ownership mark`

### Variants
juvenile broken short route patterns / adult stable long patterns / old individual layered route echoes.

### DO NOT
`mount, caravan saddle, glowing antlers, pet eyes, giant turtle-city silhouette, legendary beast armor`

## B02 종울음새 — THE LIVING ALARM

### V0 Rest Shape 4안
`medium flying sovereign beast smaller than a human torso, resonant expandable throat-chest organ, layered vibration feathers at wing edges, individual form designed for flock behavior rather than heroic single-bird profile`

4안: 공명가슴 / 진동깃 / 접힌날개 / 군집간 빈공간 관계 중심.

### Moving Prompt
`small flock spiraling around structures while changing spacing, producing a deliberate empty wedge in the flock pattern, motion communicates warning before any dramatic sound effect`

### Trace Prompt
`fine naturally shed resonance feathers, low vibration sensed in walls, sudden directional silence created by flock absence`

### Environment Variants
- urban flock: short high resonance
- cliff flock: longer low resonance
- old cathedral flock: irregular interaction with human bells

### Contract Scene
`protected empty nesting zone maintained by humans, no flags attached to birds, flock choosing distance freely`

### DO NOT
`giant phoenix, magical songbird pet, glowing feather rarity, messenger bird harness, single mascot specimen`

## B03 유리등각수 — THE MOUNTAIN LISTENER

### V0 Rest Shape 4안
`mountain sovereign beast between large horse and rhinoceros in scale, layered rock-like back line, short strong legs, skin and mineral boundary visually ambiguous, no decorative crystal armor plates`

4안: 등층 / 다리길이 / 광물-피부경계 / 저중심 정지자세 중심.

### Moving Prompt
`slow deliberate movement, placing full weight into the ground, pausing for long intervals, one deep ground press just before danger, herd stillness responding to unseen fracture vibration`

### Trace Prompt
`thin vibration dust on rock surface, low scraping sound, collective stillness before cracks, local stone colors echoed in skin without fantasy glow`

### Age Variants
juvenile less mineral layering / adult local-rock integration / old individual long repaired-looking fracture scars.

### Contract Scene
`quiet mining exclusion corridor maintained by people, beast staying nearby voluntarily, no collar or tool attached`

### DO NOT
`crystal dragon, gemstone armor, elemental earth aura, boss monster horns, giant armored rhinoceros cliché`

## B04 역조고래 — THE OTHER CALENDAR

### V0 Rest/Surface Shape 4안
`true marine giant larger than a ship but grounded in believable whale-like ocean scale, long back line, route scars accumulated across body, body mostly submerged, identity completed by current patterns around it`

### DO NOT

## B05 백지사슴 — THE PRESENT WITHOUT A PAST

### V0 Rest Shape 4안
`large real-world deer scale or slightly larger, naturalistic anatomy, no giant sacred-tree antlers, stable hoof geometry and characteristic head-turn angle, external appearance intentionally variable between observer versions`

4안은 동일 개체의 관찰판 A/B/C와 구조검증용 중립판:
- A: 뿔 가지/털패턴 1
- B: 다른 뿔 가지/털패턴 2
- C: 또 다른 털명암/귀선 3
- D: hoof/head-angle invariant study

### Moving Prompt
`choosing a route independently, stopping at a fixed distance and looking back at a consistent angle, never approaching because called by humans`

### Trace Prompt
`same hoofprints recorded by different observers despite conflicting appearance descriptions, pressed feeding grass, consistent movement line`

### Observer Set Rule
A/B/C 외형은 달라도 다음은 동일:
- hoof shape
- walking rhythm
- head-turn angle
- scale
- environment interaction

### Contract Scene
`protected feeding ground and no-tracking rule respected by humans, deer appearing somewhat more often but never accepting ownership marks`

### Break Scene
`capture attempt causing ordinary animal fear and reduced path-sensing behavior, not magical rage explosion`

### DO NOT
`pure white sacred deer, enormous divine antlers, glowing eyes, forest god aura, pet behavior, chosen hero companion, evolution variants`
