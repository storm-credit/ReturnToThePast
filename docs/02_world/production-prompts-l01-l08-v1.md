# Landmark Production Prompts — L01–L08 v1

Status: **D16.3 MODEL-AGNOSTIC PROMPT PACK**  
Rule: 배경은 postcard가 아니라 `생활행동 foreground + 제도/노동 midground + 역사/시간 landmark background`의 3층으로 제작한다.

공통 suffix:
`inhabited fantasy civic space, visible labor and administration, architectural history shown through repair and use, readable grayscale structure, no empty postcard composition, no direct real-world city copy, no franchise imitation`

## L01 아르켄 왕관수도 — LAYERS OF AUTHORITY

### Establishing 4안
공통:
`capital city seen through an old lower-port address marker in the foreground, vertical palace approval corridors rising behind, horizontal cathedral-civic complex crossing the middle, clustered observation towers connected by bridges, multiple historical foundations visible without becoming ruins`

- A: 하항 주소표 foreground 강함
- B: 수직 왕궁 vs 수평 성당 대비 강함
- C: 관측탑 군집/다리 빈공간 강함
- D: 노동자 길찾기와 승인회랑 동시행동 강함

### Materials
`white lime stone, brass, red sealing wax accents, old lower-port timber, repaired masonry`

### Life Layer
`port workers still using old place-name boards under royal symbols, clerks moving through approval corridors, patients and civic users crossing the horizontal institution layer`

### P1 Variant
`some central emblems removed, regional marks placed in parallel on the same walls, architecture survives but authority no longer visually monopolizes the frame`

### DO NOT
`generic white fantasy castle, one giant palace, cathedral postcard, perfectly clean capital, one-era architecture`
